Where the numbers come from

Prices. Futures settlements from TradingView and Yahoo Finance, a session old, plus a few prices typed in by hand and dated. Each route reads its curves for the day the cargo loads and the day it lands.

Freight. A rate reported by the Baltic Exchange or the press when there is one, and a voyage model otherwise, with hire, bunkers, port costs, canals and EU allowances.

Physical flows. Port loadings and chokepoint transits are IMF PortWatch estimates from AIS. US stocks and flows are the EIA weekly reports.

Text. The supply and demand facts and the trade ideas are written by hand, dated and sourced. Everything else is recomputed by the daily run.

Limits. These are futures and public estimates, not physical assessments. The premiums and discounts of real cargoes are missing, and so are vessel positions. Not investment advice.
